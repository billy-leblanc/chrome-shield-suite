# Safety Intercept — Positioning

*The one-line reframe: not a scam-blocking extension — the **trust & safety layer for the intelligent-finance era.***

---

## The shift

For a decade, fintech was about **access** (see and move your money). The next decade is about **intelligence + trust** — AI that understands your situation and, increasingly, acts on your behalf. The gating factor isn't capability. It's trust.

Safety Intercept lives at the single highest-trust moment in finance: **the half-second before money leaves an account.** That's the moment to detect fraud, explain the "why," and ask for a human confirm. As AI agents start moving money, that checkpoint goes from nice-to-have to required infrastructure.

## Why now — the market says so (Plaid × Harris Poll, "State of Intelligent Finance," Spring 2026)

- **Fraud detection is a top desired AI capability and the #1 trust driver.** 57% say AI helps them detect scams/fraud; **57% are more likely to trust a fintech app that uses AI to detect fraud and prevent scams.**
- **Fraud protection is the #1 reason people share data** — 41%, above cashback and better rates.
- **The agentic wave is here, and it demands guardrails.** 44% would let an AI agent move money — but require a **confirmation step (41%)**, **undo/reverse (40%)**, **human approval (44%)**, **dollar limits (32%)**. The mandate: *"stake-matched guardrails — security must scale with the risk level of the action."*
- **Trust = transparency + explainability.** 75% want to know when AI is used; **60% trust more when they understand the "why."** *"A privacy policy doesn't build trust. A product that behaves predictably and honestly does."*
- **Accountability is expected.** 80% believe companies should make AI mistakes right.

Every one of those is something Safety Intercept already does or is built to do.

## What we are

**Consumer (the wedge):** a free Chrome extension that pauses you before you send money to a scammer — plain-English warning, you decide, we help you recover if one gets through.

**Business (the company):** the same engine as an **embeddable trust & safety layer** — fraud detection + explainability + a confirm-before-you-send checkpoint — plus a **licensable real-time scam-domain feed** (registrar, age, hosting, TLS/kit fingerprints, campaign attribution). Permissioned and PII-free by design.

## The moat

1. **Position at the moment of action** — the guardrail every agentic-payment flow has to plug into.
2. **Explainability built in** — the "why," which the data says converts trust into adoption.
3. **Permissioned, PII-free architecture** — trust as a structural feature, not a policy page.
4. **A self-feeding threat-intelligence dataset** — detections → registry → feed → more detections.

## The bet

We've been building a **scam blocker for individuals.** The durable opportunity is being the **trust-and-safety guardrail the entire agentic-finance wave plugs into.** Same code. Much bigger game.
