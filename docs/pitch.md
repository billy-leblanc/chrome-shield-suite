# Safety Intercept — Pitch

## One-liner
**"Using AI to fight AI."**

## The Problem
Scammers are using AI to generate personalized, convincing fraud at scale.
Grandparent scams, romance scams, fake emergencies — all crafted by AI,
targeting real people at the moment they're about to send money.

## The Solution
Safety Intercept intercepts payments in real time and uses AI to analyze
the memo before money moves. If it looks like a scam, we stop it.

## Why Now
Banks' fraud systems were built for card skimming.
They were not built for the AI scam era.

## The Market
$10B+ lost to payment scams annually in the US alone.
Every bank, credit union, and fintech has this problem.

## The Product
- Chrome extension as proof of concept and demo
- Real-time AI scoring via Cloudflare + Anthropic
- 7 platforms supported: PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, Wells Fargo, HSBC, Barclays, Revolut
- 100% critical fraud detection rate in testing

## The Real Business
B2B API licensed to banks and fintechs.
One enterprise contract > 100,000 free extension users.
Target customer: VP of Risk at a fintech company.

## YC Story
Built a working demo, deployed it on real payment platforms,
caught real fraud patterns, and built the data infrastructure
to prove it works at scale.
