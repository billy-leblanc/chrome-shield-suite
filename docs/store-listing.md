### Long Description

**Protect yourself from social engineering scams and payment fraud, instantly.**

Online scams are getting smarter. AI-generated fraud and social engineering tactics are harder to spot than ever. **Safety Intercept** is your personal security guard for online payments, designed to catch what you might miss before you hit "Send."

### 🛡️ Real-Time Shield
Whether you're using PayPal, Venmo, or Zelle, Safety Intercept monitors your payment actions in real-time. If you're about to send money based on a suspicious request, we intercept it before it's too late.

### 🤖 AI-Driven Analysis
Our advanced Risk Engine uses state-of-the-art AI to analyze payment memos and notes. It detects patterns common in grandparent scams, prize fraud, and urgency-based social engineering, providing you with a clear risk score and actionable advice.

### 🔒 Privacy-First Design
Your security shouldn't come at the cost of your privacy.
- **Zero Data Harvesting:** We never store your payment info or account numbers.
- **Local Stats:** Your security logs stay on your device.
- **No Personal Tracking:** We only activate our shield when you're on a payment portal.

### 🚀 Key Features:
- **Instant Risk Scoring:** See exactly why a transaction is flagged.
- **Heuristic & AI Detection:** Multi-layer protection against known and emerging threats.
- **Universal Portal Support:** Protected zones for PayPal, Venmo, Zelle, and major banking portals.
- **Personal Security Dashboard:** Track your protection stats and view saved intercept logs.

Don't let scammers win. Take control of your digital safety with **Safety Intercept**.
