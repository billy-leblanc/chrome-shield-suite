\section{The Core Thesis}

Every fraud company in the market answers the same question: \textit{was this transaction fraudulent?} They evaluate after the fact --- after the money moved, after the damage is done.

Safety Intercept answers a different question: \textit{should this transaction ever happen?}

That is not a feature improvement. It is a different position in the stack entirely. Traditional fraud systems operate at step 3. Safety Intercept operates at step 0 --- before the user hits Send, with context from their inbox. The timing advantage is the moat.

\section{The Category}

\textbf{Safety Intercept is the first real-time scam interception layer.}

This is not marketing language. It is a category claim. The word \textit{interception} is precise --- it means we are physically inside the transaction, not watching from outside. The word \textit{layer} signals infrastructure to every fintech and neobank that reads it.

Nobody has planted this flag. We planted it.

\section{The Business Model}

\textbf{Two-door, one flywheel.}

\subsection{Door 1 --- Consumer}

Free Chrome extension. No subscription. Installs on PayPal, Wells Fargo Zelle, and Gmail. Every install generates labeled fraud signal. Every blocked payment is a labeled positive. Every completed safe payment is a labeled negative. This is the best training data for payment-authorization fraud that exists --- if you scale it.

\subsection{Door 2 --- B2B API}

The dataset becomes the product. \texttt{POST /score\_memo} takes a payment memo and returns a scam probability score. Stripe does not offer this as a standalone service. Sardine built their dataset from customer-reported fraud --- lagging and noisy. Safety Intercept's dataset is labeled at the moment of interception --- real-time and precise.

The B2B pitch at scale: \textit{``Our model has seen 500,000 scam memos. Plaid's has only seen payment volumes.''}

Target buyers: Chime, Varo, Current, SoFi, local credit unions. Entry point: Strawberry Creek Ventures (Berkeley alumni fund) for warm introductions.

\subsection{The Flywheel}

Free consumer tool $\rightarrow$ labeled interceptions $\rightarrow$ proprietary dataset $\rightarrow$ B2B API $\rightarrow$ revenue funds better model $\rightarrow$ better model improves consumer tool $\rightarrow$ more installs. It compounds.

\section{The Competitive Position}

\textbf{Nobody is doing exactly what we are doing.} The combination --- browser extension + Gmail cross-layer correlation + Send button interception on PayPal and Zelle --- is ours alone.

\subsection{Whitespace We Own}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The 24-hour cross-layer correlation window.} Scam email $\rightarrow$ payment attempt, same device, tight causal window. No competitor has this signal.
  \item \textbf{The Send button surface on P2P platforms.} Scamnetic verifies recipients inside the FI. Norton scans the inbox. Google blocks phishing sites. Nobody intercepts at the actual Send button inside PayPal or Zelle.
  \item \textbf{Payment memo scoring as a standalone API.} Stripe does not offer it. Nobody does.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Threat Levels}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textcolor{red}{\textbf{RED --- Scamnetic.}} \$16M raised, same consumer$\rightarrow$B2B playbook, 12 months ahead. Their constraint: mobile app, not browser. No Gmail cross-layer correlation. Our wedge is real.
  \item \textcolor{red}{\textbf{RED --- Google Chrome Gemini Nano.}} On-device LLM scam detection, roadmap expanding. Defense: the dataset and the correlation signal. Not the extension itself.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Structural Advantages Incumbents Cannot Replicate}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Plaid cannot build a consumer Chrome extension --- it destroys their B2B neutrality
  \item Aura cannot build technical interception --- they are a marketing company
  \item Stripe Radar is locked inside the Stripe ecosystem
\end{itemize}

\section{What We Have Built (April 2026)}

\subsection{Three Live Surfaces}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PayPal} --- Send button interception, proactive memo pre-analysis, SPA-aware
  \item \textbf{Wells Fargo Zelle} --- Hash-route-aware interception on SENDMONEY\_ENTER\_DETAILS and SENDMONEY\_VERIFY\_DETAILS
  \item \textbf{Gmail} --- Scam email detection, real-time banner, feeds 24-hour correlation window
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Intelligence Stack}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Heuristic fraud detector --- 7 scam type patterns
  \item Claude Haiku LLM analysis --- $\sim$500ms, 80\% weight on Gmail, 40\% on payment
  \item Cross-layer correlation --- Gmail scam event + payment attempt within 24h = +30 risk score
  \item Keyword correlation --- memo text matched against scam email keywords
  \item Questionnaire trance-breaker --- shown before AI analysis on every interception
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Infrastructure}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Cloudflare Workers relay: auth, LLM proxy, events, telemetry
  \item Cloudflare KV: SHIELD\_LOGS (operational) + TELEMETRY\_LOGS (training data, opt-in)
  \item MV3 service worker with keepalive via \texttt{chrome.alarms} every 20 seconds
  \item Shadow DOM isolation on all injected UI
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Viral Loop}

Post-intercept share screen triggers after ``Go back --- stay safe.'' Shows blocked amount. Three share channels: WhatsApp, SMS, copy link. Share text is pre-written and includes the download link. People who just got saved from a scam are the most motivated sharers.
