Common questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?
Yes — Safety Intercept is completely free to install and use. No trial, no credit card, no hidden upgrade to "unlock" protection.
Can you see my bank account or move my money?
No. Safety Intercept reads the payment or email on the page you're already looking at to spot scam patterns. It has no access to your bank, your balance, or your ability to move money.
What does it actually check?
It looks at the things scammers rely on: brand-new recipients, urgent "pay now" pressure, spoofed sender addresses, suspicious links, and gift-card or wire requests — then explains what it found in plain English.
Will it slow me down or get in the way?
No. It stays invisible until it sees something risky. When it does step in, you can dismiss the warning in one tap and carry on.
Which sites does it support?
Today: PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Wells Fargo / Zelle, and Gmail. More banks and email providers are on the way.
