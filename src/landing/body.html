How it works

It reads the room, not your data.

Safety Intercept runs locally in your browser and only looks at the payment or message in front of you — the instant it matters.

01 · WATCH

Sees the moment

When you're about to send a payment or open a suspicious email, the extension reads what's on screen — the memo, the recipient, the message.

02 · SCORE

Weighs the signals

A hybrid engine checks dozens of known scam patterns — urgency, secrecy, impersonation, lookalike domains — and assigns a risk score in real time.

03 · INTERCEPT

Stops you in time

If it's risky, you get a clear warning before the money leaves — with the specific reasons, so you can decide with your eyes open.
